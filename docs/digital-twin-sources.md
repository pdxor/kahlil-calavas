## Images

Existing tour and timeline images are retained, including the previously hosted ChoZen, Downtown Mall, and Monticello images. Three WebP images were copied unchanged from the corresponding project’s existing public media. These are digital-model visualizations, described as such in their alt text:

- `public/tours/uva-contemplative-commons.webp`: UVA tour, `/media/01_Aerial.webp`.
- `public/tours/carter-mountain.webp`: Carter Mountain tour, `/media/orchard-overview.webp`.
- `public/tours/preserve-life.webp`: Preserve Life twin, `/media/01_Estate_Aerial.webp`.

No 3D models, panoramas, or video files are duplicated into this portfolio. Visitors open the source tour to experience its latest published version.
